The Data Protection Risk Game on paper

Cards, coins and dice at a table. Two or more teams and a facilitator.

  • Rulebook
    How to set up and play, one part at a time. Start here.
  • Player aid
    One page for every team. Print it and keep it on the table.
  • Facilitator sheet
    For the person who runs the game and does not play.
  • The cards (PDF, 14 MB)
    Print double-sided, flip on the long edge, cut at the marks.
  • The cards, text only (PDF, 2 MB)
    The same cards without pictures. Lighter to print.
  • Risk matrix (PDF)
    One per risk and team. Print a stack.

This is a playtest version: the numbers in square brackets are proposals.

Start page · Play online

Loosely based on the German Standard Data Protection Model (SDM) and NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5. An independent adaptation for learning: not endorsed by either, and not legal advice. NIST references on the cards are related controls, not equivalents.

Quelle: Konferenz der unabhängigen Datenschutzaufsichtsbehörden des Bundes und der Länder (Datenschutzkonferenz), SDM V3.1. Verändert: Maßnahmen gekürzt, ins Englische übertragen und um Spielwerte ergänzt. Datenlizenz Deutschland – Namensnennung – Version 2.0.
