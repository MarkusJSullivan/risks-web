Facilitator sheet

1. Start of the project

  1. Cards. Deal up: check that every team has [9] cards in hand, the blue cards on the table included. Teams turn their blue and yellow cards upright.
  2. Coins. Pay each team [10]. First project: plus [20] starting coins, [30] in all. Your power: more generous = longer game, stricter = shorter. Same for every team. Make up reasons: "Budget freeze", "Economy is taking off".
  3. Risks. Turn over risk cards: [1] in the first project. Optional, for experienced groups: then [2], [2], [3], and again from [1]. Beginners do better with fewer risks. Short story. Same risks for every team.
  4. Matrices and figures. One matrix per risk. Risk card circle: left number = probability, right number = impact. Put the figure of that colour on that space (see matrix).
  5. Yellow cards. Every upright yellow card still has to make its moves, once, on the new matrices. Then it is turned sideways. None in the first project.

2. Each of the four turns

Teams (one action each, all at once): play a card, pay its coins, move figures on every matrix. Or swap up to [5] cards, free (not sideways blue cards). Your power: 1 or 2 coins when a team explains well why its measure fits the risk. Nothing for a funny story that does not fit.

  • Grey: discard, draw a new card.
  • Blue: face up and turned sideways next to the team, no draw. Once per project. Still part of the hand. Face up = played before, so replay price (full price again if it went to the discard pile).
  • Yellow: face up on the table, turned sideways (moves made this project), draw. Moves now.

You: check the moves. After all teams: draw an event card, read it out, applies to all. "Lose one action" = turn lost, "gain one action" = extra turn. Draw pile empty: reshuffle discard. Events can change the number of turns. Mark the turn with a coin on the turn track of the matrix.

  • New goal (EV40, EV41): pick one risk card at random; every team adds the first goal by letter (A C I L M T V) it lacks, on 3 / 3. Only later cards move it.
  • Reset (EV39): all cards of this project and all yellow cards are gone; figures back to the start (new goal 3 / 3); coins stay spent.
  • Wing it (EV38): each team plays 3 blind cards, not the sideways blue ones; no turns used; cannot pay = project ends. End the project (EV17): DPO call first, then roll.

3. End of the project

a) Optional: the DPO (before rolling)

Optional rule. Ask before anybody rolls. A team with a figure in the red pays [1] coin (not refunded) for a DPO, who argues to stop the project. Roll your d20 in secret: [10] or above = management open to it, a good argument beats a low roll. Your power: yes if the argument names a real weakness, the roll is only a guide.
  • Bought: no roll, no check, no cost this round. The risk comes back next round: same matrices and figures, full turns again, on top of the new risks. Blue and yellow cards are not played on the moved matrices: only grey cards move their figures.
  • Not bought: continue as normal with b) Roll. The coin is lost.

b) Roll

Each team rolls its d20 once, in the open. Safe if the roll is the probability row number or above.

c) Cost and story

Per failed figure: cost = impact (column) squared, in coins. You make up the story. Cannot pay = out of business.

ImpactCostThe story
1[1] A complaint. Someone writes in, you answer.
2[4] Report to the authority. Lawyers and forms.
3[9] Inquiry by the authority. Lawyers, forced changes.
4[16] People get hurt. Lawsuit, damages, newspapers.
Optional rule. A team that cannot pay argues to management it was not their fault. You play management. Roll your d20 in secret as a guide: [10] or above = open to covering, a good argument beats a low roll. May cover some or all of the fee. Your power: cover part if its measures fitted its risks, none if they were scattered.

4. Next project

Back to step 1: blue and yellow cards are turned upright there, also after a bought DPO.
